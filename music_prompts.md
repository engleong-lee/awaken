# 醒来 (Awaken) - AI Music Generation Prompts

> Background music prompts for each episode. Use with Suno, Beatoven.ai, SOUNDRAW, or Mubert.
> 
> **General Settings:**
> - Duration: 15 minutes (loop-friendly)
> - Style: Ambient, atmospheric, lo-fi, minimal
> - No vocals, instrumental only
> - **NO DRUMS, NO PERCUSSION, NO BEATS** - pure ambient
> - Suitable for late-night listening
>
> **Suno Duration Notes:**
> - Suno V4.5/V5 generates up to 8 minutes max per generation
> - Use "Extend" feature to add more length
> - For background music, generate ~4-8 min and loop in your audio editor
>
> **Alternative for exact duration:** Use **Mubert** - it allows specifying exact track length (e.g., 15 minutes)

## Key Phrases to ALWAYS Include (Suno)

```
no drums, no percussion, no beats, beatless
```

This is critical - Suno defaults to adding rhythm. You must explicitly exclude it.

---

## Tips for Generation

1. **Always include "no drums, no percussion, no beats, beatless"** - this is essential
2. **Use words like:** drone, pad, ambient, atmospheric, floating, spacious
3. **Avoid words like:** groove, rhythm, beat, tempo, BPM (these trigger percussion)
4. **Test with voice** - Play your voiceover on top to check it doesn't compete
5. **Consistent volume** - Background music should sit at -20dB to -15dB under voice
6. **Fade in/out** - Add 3-5 second fades at start and end of each episode
7. **Looping** - Use crossfade (2-3 seconds) when looping tracks in Audacity/GarageBand
8. **Regenerate** - If Suno still adds drums, regenerate - it's somewhat random
